## Editorial and ethical treatment

The harp, gate, home and constellation route are original dramatic
illustrations, not evidence or reconstructions of a historical scene. The
Psalm 137 beat uses silence, water and an instrument only: it does not show
violence, captives or suffering as spectacle. The accessible caption track is
`del-polvo-a-la-alabanza-v2.es.vtt`; a static final poster is supplied beside
the clip for reduced-motion use.

## Sound

Instrumental Noche cues only: `round_open`, `timer_tension`, `dramatic_fire`,
`round_lock`, `reveal`, `fire_whoosh`, and `royal_fanfare`. No speech, lyrics,
choir or organ are present.
