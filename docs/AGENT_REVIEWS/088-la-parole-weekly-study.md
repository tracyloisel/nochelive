## Feeling

Curiosity becomes reassurance, then understanding, accomplishment and belonging. The score is never presented as spiritual worth.

## 1 — Game experience

The loop is scripture reference → meaningful choice → immediate reveal → short spiritual explanation → visible light → next question → final sentence to keep. The weekly journey has ten distinct ideas and ends in a ceremony rather than a quiet result row.

## 2 — UI design

The home keeps one gold action for the main adventure; La Parole is a navy secondary invitation. Programme, week, journey and community use the player HUD plus the Aventure dock. The focused quiz deliberately removes the dock and keeps a compact reference / ten-light / score HUD. The reader remains modal and the ceremony protects its payoff before offering exits.

## 3 — Art direction

The Psalms use Celestial Dark: an ancient pilgrim, storm, rock refuge and one volumetric gold opening. Completion changes to Celestial Light through a stone gateway at dawn. Both generated artworks have no text, logos, modern objects or literal temple photography.

## Theme engine

The existing Home remains one markup tree. The weekly card consumes the current semantic gold/dark surface tokens and its own narrative artwork; no theme toggle and no duplicated Home.

## Four seats

N/A street journey. Who: player HUD remains. Where: weekly Psalms journey. What now: start/continue one step. Around me: parish participation without ranking faith.

## Tension

Observation rises toward Christ, repentance, trial, memory, righteous rule and reconciliation. Explanation payoff follows each answer; the final light synthesizes the week.

## Finale

Question ten resolves mercy, truth, righteousness and peace, then opens a gateway ceremony with the immutable 10-question result and the weekly light.

## Languages

Generic UI and all ten questions read in es, pt-BR, en and fr. French uses `tu` for the solo player; Spanish is warm; Brazilian Portuguese uses `ala`; English avoids quiz-show language.

## Verdict

PASS WITH NOTES

## What works

- The annual source, 52 weeks, eight reflection resources and four appendixes are first-class, auditable records.
- The current week is a real authored spiritual journey, not generated trivia.
- Immutable quiz versions protect historical answers.
- Parish progress celebrates companionship rather than claiming to measure faith.
- « Mon parcours » exposes all 52 weeks as a chronological timeline with completed, open, current and upcoming states.
- A present-week card precedes the long timeline: receive the light when new, continue when open, celebrate and share when completed.
- CSS View Transitions keep the quiz world and HUD stable while the question sheet changes; answer, reader and ceremony motion all honor reduced motion.

## What feels weak

- Only the current week has a published editorial quiz; the other imported weeks honestly show their review state.
- Free chapter exploration is the next vertical slice, not hidden behind a dead button.

## Required before approval

- None for the current-week vertical slice.
